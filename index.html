Privacy Policy

Your privacy is as important to us as your cats. This policy outlines how your data is handled in the 2026 version of Luna's World.

Data Infrastructure

We use Firebase for all data storage. Your information is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols. We never sell your personal data.

Core Features & Privacy

  • Breed Detection: Image analysis happens locally on your device via CoreML. Your photos are never uploaded for AI training.
  • Location Data: We use coarse location data (Cities) to filter clinics and adoption posts. We do not track your movement.
  • User Profiles: Email and ID data are stored solely to facilitate account security and post management.

Your Rights

You may delete your posts directly within the app. For full account deletion and data removal, please email us using the link above.
